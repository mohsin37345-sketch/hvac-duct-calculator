## What Is a Ductulator

A ductulator is a circular slide rule tool that HVAC professionals have used for decades to look up duct sizes. You align the CFM and friction rate scales, and the tool shows the corresponding duct diameter, velocity, and rectangular equivalent.

Physical ductulators are still manufactured and widely used on job sites. They require no batteries, no internet connection, and fit in a shirt pocket.

## What Is an Online Duct Calculator

An online duct calculator performs the same fundamental calculations as a ductulator but in digital form. It takes inputs like CFM, friction rate, duct length, and fittings, and outputs the duct size, velocity, and pressure loss.

## Advantages of a Physical Ductulator

### Works Anywhere
In a crawl space with no cell signal, on a roof in the rain, or in a mechanical room without WiFi. The physical tool always works.

### Tactile and Visual
Experienced professionals can quickly scan across scales to see how changing one parameter affects others. The visual nature of the slide rule makes relationships between variables intuitive.

### No Distractions
No notifications, no pop ups, no ads. Just the duct sizing data you need.

### Industry Standard Credibility
Presenting a ductulator to a client or inspector carries professional credibility. It shows you are using the same tool the industry has trusted for decades.

## Advantages of an Online Calculator

### Higher Accuracy
Digital calculations eliminate reading errors from misaligned scales or parallax. The result is computed to decimal precision.

### Automatic TEL
Online calculators include fitting count fields and automatically calculate Total Effective Length, which ductulators cannot do.

### Metal and Flex Adjustments
Our calculator automatically adjusts friction calculations for flex duct, eliminating the manual correction step required with a ductulator.

### Additional Features
- Rectangular to round conversion
- Diameter to rectangular conversion
- Multiple sizing methods (friction rate and velocity)
- Instant results for different scenarios

### Always Available
Any phone, tablet, or computer can access an online calculator. No tool to forget at the office.

## When to Use Each

### Use a Ductulator When:
- Working in areas without internet access
- Quick job site verification needed
- Client or inspector expects to see one
- Personal preference for physical tools

### Use an Online Calculator When:
- Designing a complete system at a desk
- Needing precise calculations with TEL included
- Comparing multiple duct size options quickly
- Working with flex duct (automatic adjustments)
- Training or learning duct sizing concepts

### Best Approach: Use Both
Many professionals keep a ductulator in their tool bag for field work and use an [online calculator](/) for design work at the office. The two tools complement each other.
